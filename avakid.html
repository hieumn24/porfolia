The setup

AVAKid sells kids' products and AVASport sells sports equipment, both under the MWG group. They share a technical base but not an audience, so the useful question was which decisions could be made once and applied to both, and which had to be made twice.

What was achieved

  • AVAKid grew to 45K daily visits with strong rankings in kids' retail
  • AVASport reached 15K daily visits in a competitive sports market
  • Managed 5 PBNs for authority building and link equity
  • Implemented off-page entity strategies for brand recognition
  • Ran full audits and speed optimization on both properties

Approach

  • Dual-site content strategy tailored to two different audiences
  • PBN network management for link building
  • Entity SEO for brand authority
  • Technical audits and performance work, shared where the stack was shared
  • Product page optimization for conversion
  • Category-specific keyword targeting

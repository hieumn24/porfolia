The Setup

BitJoy sells eSIM data plans worldwide on Shopify. 1,769 products, 194 collections, 11 locales. The competitors are Airalo and Holafly. Airalo gets 246,000 brand searches a month, BitJoy gets 50. There is no paid channel and no app, so everything below came out of organic search.

What the numbers did

  • Organic clicks: 5,359 in Oct 2025, 29,947 in Apr 2026.
  • 54,258 keywords rank. 39,941 of them are in the top 10, 11,168 in the top 3.
  • Thirteen of those keywords are branded. The other 54,245 are not.
  • Ten language markets, none of them under 500 clicks per 28 days. No single market carries the site.
  • Revenue up 3.9x, but on 4x AOV, not more orders. Order count stayed flat the whole time. Worth knowing before anyone reads growth into it.
  • 203 articles published in the first eight months, 374 live now.

Where I decided not to fight

  • Dropped "esim {country}" as a target. Those SERPs belong to Airalo, Holafly and the aggregators, and brand search predicts who wins them. Went after the local carrier name instead: djezzy, cosmote, bh telecom, a1, claro.
  • Found 33 commercial queries where the blog post and the collection were both ranking for the same thing. Usually the blog sat at position 2 and the collection at 50. Fixed the split rather than building more links to the collection.
  • English root only. The translation layer pushes changes into 11 locales on its own, so writing per-locale content was never worth the cost.
  • Blog is 98% of traffic and converts at 0.03%. Stopped reporting it as a revenue line and started measuring it as reach. That was an unpopular slide.
  • Built 8 "30 days" collections off duration modifiers people actually search, taken from Search Console, not from a keyword tool's suggestions.

Technical work

  • Site-wide position dropped 1.5 on 20 Apr 2026, uniform across blog and locales. Traced it to three theme PRs merged 14-15 Apr that made the global stylesheet async and deleted the font-face block. Reverted in PR #979. Position went from 9.00 back to 7.93.
  • 258 "No global identifier" errors appeared in Search Console. They were my fault: the collection ItemList snippet I shipped had offers but no brand, sku or mpn. Added them in PR #951.
  • 131 "missing image" product errors turned out to be 112 products using an SVG as the featured image. Google will not accept SVG for product schema. Rasterised to WebP and swapped every one.
  • GA4 reported 92 purchases against Shopify's 155. Matched them order by order over a settled week: 57% captured. The accepted explanation was the crypto gateway, but Solana landed best at 71% and Stripe worst at 54%, so the loss was client-side consent and adblock, not the gateway. Fix went server-side instead.
  • Mobile Lighthouse 52. Images were only 196KB, the slider library was 9.3s of CPU by itself. Lazy-mounted it behind an IntersectionObserver in PR #981.
  • When orders dropped to 0-1-2 a day, the funnel was intact. Two Solana wholesale regulars had stopped buying after a catalog-wide price rise. They were 36 of 66 orders that month.

Doing it at catalog scale

  • Every bulk change runs through Python against the Shopify GraphQL API: dry-run first, back up the field, write, then assert the change actually landed. Nothing gets edited by hand at this volume.
  • 144 collections rebuilt. Each gets its own carriers, 5G cities and airports plus 8 FAQ entries, so no two country pages read the same.
  • 455 product titles corrected, 1,307 meta descriptions generated from live price and plan data.
  • 1,769 products filled with Shopify category metafields, seven fields each.
  • 1,109 images across 161 articles had the article title as their alt text, on every image in the post. Rewrote them from the surrounding heading and caption.
  • Monthly client report pulls Search Console, GA4 and Shopify into one file.

What Is Still Open

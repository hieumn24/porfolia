Project Overview

BitJoy is a global eSIM store on Shopify: 1,769 products, 194 collections and 11 language locales, competing against Airalo, Holafly, Saily and Nomad. Organic search is the only acquisition channel, so every click, ranking and order traces back to SEO. I own strategy, technical SEO, content operations and measurement end to end.

🎯 Key Achievements

  • Organic clicks grew from 5,359 to 29,947 per month (+459%) between Oct 2025 and Apr 2026
  • Built a portfolio of 54,258 ranking keywords, of which 39,941 sit in the top 10 and 11,168 in the top 3
  • 99.98% of keywords are non-branded: only 13 branded queries in the whole set, so rankings are earned by content and technical work, not brand demand
  • Ranking simultaneously in 10 language markets, each producing four-digit monthly clicks
  • Store revenue grew 3.9x over the same period, driven by average order value up 4x rather than order volume
  • Published 203 articles in the first 8 months, growing the live library to 374 articles

🔍 SEO Strategy

  • Carrier-term targeting instead of head terms: the generic "esim {country}" SERPs are locked by brands with 3 to 4 orders of magnitude more brand search, so I targeted local carrier names where the SERP is winnable
  • English-first multilingual model: optimise the English root, let the translation layer propagate to 11 locales, avoiding per-locale rewrite cost
  • Page-type discipline: resolved blog vs collection cannibalization across 33 commercial queries where the site was competing with itself
  • Long-tail collection architecture: 8 duration-modifier smart collections built from measured Search Console demand
  • Intent-based prioritisation: separated high-traffic informational clusters from the buy-intent subset and measured them on different KPIs
  • Competitive benchmarking against 4 direct competitors covering pricing, product gaps, review moat and content depth

⚙️ Technical SEO

  • Root-caused a site-wide 1.5 position drop to the exact date and the exact 3 theme pull requests that caused it (async CSS plus deleted font-face declarations), shipped the fix and recovered from position 9.00 back to 7.93
  • Rebuilt the collection schema stack: ItemList, BreadcrumbList and Organization with brand, sku and mpn, clearing 258 "No global identifier" errors in Search Console
  • Fixed 131 product schema image errors by tracing them to 112 products using SVG featured images, then rasterising and swapping every one
  • Proved a 43% analytics blind spot: order-level matching of Shopify against GA4 showed only 57% of orders were captured, disproving the accepted "payment gateway" explanation and redirecting the fix to server-side tracking
  • Performance diagnosis: identified the slider library as the top CPU cost at 9.3s and app-injected inline JavaScript as 64% of a 1MB page, and shipped the lazy-init fix
  • Diagnosed a sales drop as a pricing decision rather than a funnel fault, by isolating two wholesale repeat buyers lost to a catalog-wide price increase

🤖 Automation and Scale

  • Built a Python and Shopify GraphQL pipeline to run SEO changes at catalog scale, with dry-run, backup and assertion checks before every write
  • 144 collections rebuilt with unique carrier, city and airport level content plus 8 FAQ entries each
  • 455 product titles corrected and 1,307 meta descriptions generated from live price and plan data
  • 1,769 products populated with Shopify standard category metafields, 7 fields each, zero errors
  • 1,109 images across 161 articles given unique contextual alt text, replacing duplicated title-as-alt from a previous contributor
  • Automated reporting pulling Search Console, GA4 and Shopify into a single monthly client deliverable

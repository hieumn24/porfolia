The setup

BitJoy sells eSIM data plans worldwide on Shopify. 1,769 products, 194 collections, 11 locales. The competitors are Airalo and Holafly. Airalo gets 246,000 brand searches a month, BitJoy gets 50. There is no paid channel and no mobile app, so everything below came out of organic search.

Where I decided not to fight

  • Dropped "esim {country}" as a target. Those SERPs belong to Airalo, Holafly and the aggregators, and brand search predicts who wins them. Went after the local carrier name instead: djezzy, cosmote, bh telecom, a1, claro.
  • Found 33 commercial queries where the blog post and the collection were both ranking for the same thing. Usually the blog sat at position 2 and the collection at 50. Fixed the split rather than building more links.
  • English root only. The translation layer pushes changes into 11 locales on its own, so writing per-locale content was never worth the cost.
  • Blog is 98% of traffic and converts at 0.03%. Stopped reporting it as a revenue line and started measuring it as reach. That was an unpopular slide.
  • Built 8 "30 days" collections off duration modifiers people actually search, taken from Search Console rather than a keyword tool's suggestions.

Technical work

  • Site-wide position dropped 1.5 on 20 Apr 2026, uniform across blog and locales. Traced it to three theme PRs merged 14-15 Apr that made the global stylesheet async and deleted the font-face block. Reverted in PR #979. Position went 9.00 back to 7.93.
  • 258 "No global identifier" errors appeared in Search Console. They were my fault: the collection ItemList snippet I shipped had offers but no brand, sku or mpn. Added them in PR #951.
  • 131 "missing image" product errors turned out to be 112 products using an SVG as the featured image. Google will not accept SVG for product schema. Rasterised to WebP and swapped every one.
  • GA4 reported 92 purchases against Shopify's 155. Matched them order by order over a settled week: 57% captured. The accepted explanation was the crypto gateway, but Solana landed best at 71% and Stripe worst at 54%, so the loss was client-side consent and adblock. Fix went server-side.
  • Mobile Lighthouse 52. Images were only 196KB; the slider library was 9.3s of CPU on its own. Lazy-mounted it behind an IntersectionObserver in PR #981.
  • When orders fell to 0-1-2 a day, the funnel was intact. Two Solana wholesale regulars had stopped after a catalog-wide price rise. They were 36 of 66 orders that month.

  • Revenue up 3.9x from the first month to the June peak.
  • That came from average order value, not volume. AOV rose 5.5x while orders stayed between 95 and 238 a month with no trend. Customers moved to bigger regional and global plans.
  • Repeat customers are 34-52% of orders in recent months, so the base compounds rather than churns.
  • Two things worth saying out loud: order volume did not grow, and a single wholesale buyer leaving can move a month by half. This is a small store, and the numbers behave like one.

What is still open

Collections average position 28 and products 17.9. On-page is done on both, and more content has not moved them. The ceiling is off-page: 36 Trustpilot reviews against competitors with 22,000 to 100,000, and no press. That is the next problem, and it is not a content problem.

Organic clicks have been flat around 29K since April. Getting past that needs authority, not more articles.

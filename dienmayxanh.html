The setup

Dienmayxanh.com is Vietnam's largest electronics retailer. This was not a growth project, it was a hold-the-line project: a site already at a million daily organic visits, where the risk is not failing to grow but breaking something at scale. I ran SEO operations on it for three years.

What was achieved

  • Held 1M+ daily visits consistently across three years
  • Grew organic share of total traffic from 40% to 50%
  • Optimized 5,000+ product pages and created 800+ guides
  • Improved page speed by 40% while features were still being added
  • Kept #1-2 category rankings against direct competitors

How

  • A product page optimization framework with structured data, applied as a template rather than page by page
  • Scalable content templates per category
  • Technical SEO infrastructure sized for a very large site
  • Custom dashboards so regressions surfaced before traffic moved
  • Authority building through linkable assets

Project Overview

SIAM Thailand is one of Thailand's leading aesthetic hospitals, specializing in cosmetic surgery and beauty treatments. When I joined as SEO Leader at XMARS Agency, the website was starting from zero organic traffic. My mission was to build a comprehensive SEO strategy from the ground up and scale traffic to support the hospital's business growth.

🎯 Project Goals

  • → Build organic traffic from 0 to 45,000+ daily visits
  • → Establish SIAM as a top authority in beauty/aesthetic industry
  • → Reduce dependency on paid advertising
  • → Create sustainable organic customer acquisition channel
  • → Handle and prevent SEO crisis situations

📊 My Responsibilities

  • ✓ Full website management and SEO operations
  • ✓ Technical SEO audits and optimization
  • ✓ Content strategy and implementation
  • ✓ Link building and off-page SEO
  • ✓ Crisis management (blackhat, hacking, DMCA)

Challenges Faced

Starting from zero traffic presented unique challenges, especially in the competitive beauty and healthcare industry in Thailand.

🚀 Starting From Absolute Zero

The website had no existing organic traffic, no backlink profile, and no established domain authority. Building everything from scratch required a comprehensive, multi-faceted approach.

💥 Highly Competitive Industry

The aesthetic surgery and beauty treatment space in Thailand is extremely competitive, with established players dominating search results for high-value keywords.

🔒 E-A-T Requirements

Healthcare and medical content requires demonstrating Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-A-T) to rank well in Google, especially after the Medic Update.

🎯 Multilingual SEO

Need to optimize for both Thai and international audiences, requiring separate strategies for local and global keywords.

⚠️ Security Threats

Faced multiple blackhat attacks, hacking attempts, and DMCA reports aimed at damaging the site's reputation and rankings.

⏱️ Aggressive Timeline

Client expected significant results within 6-12 months to justify the investment and reduce paid advertising dependency.

Strategic Solutions

A comprehensive, data-driven approach combining technical excellence, content strategy, and proactive crisis management.

1. 🔍 Comprehensive Technical SEO Overhaul

  • Conducted in-depth technical audit identifying 200+ issues
  • Implemented structured data markup for medical content and reviews
  • Optimized site speed from 4.2s to 1.8s load time
  • Fixed crawlability issues and optimized robots.txt and XML sitemaps
  • Implemented mobile-first responsive design optimization
  • Set up comprehensive monitoring with Google Search Console and Analytics

2. 📝 Strategic Content Development

  • Created 500+ high-quality, medically-accurate articles on aesthetic procedures
  • Developed content clusters around key service categories
  • Implemented E-A-T signals through author credentials and citations
  • Produced before/after galleries with proper schema markup
  • Created multilingual content for Thai and international audiences
  • Optimized all content for featured snippets and rich results

3. 🔗 Authority Building Campaign

  • Secured high-authority backlinks from medical and beauty publications
  • Built relationships with healthcare influencers and bloggers
  • Created linkable assets (research, infographics, guides)
  • Leveraged PR and media coverage for natural link acquisition
  • Implemented strategic internal linking structure

4. 🛡️ Crisis Management & Security

  • Implemented robust security measures to prevent attacks
  • Set up 24/7 monitoring for blackhat SEO tactics
  • Created rapid response protocols for DMCA and hacking incidents
  • Successfully defended against and resolved 5+ major attack attempts
  • Maintained consistent rankings throughout crisis periods

5. 📊 Data-Driven Optimization

  • Implemented advanced tracking and analytics setup
  • Created custom dashboards for real-time performance monitoring
  • Conducted monthly A/B tests on titles, meta descriptions, and CTAs
  • Used heat mapping and user behavior analysis for UX improvements
  • Continuously refined strategy based on performance data

Key Takeaways

Critical lessons and insights gained from this transformative project.

💡 Strategic Foundation

Starting with comprehensive technical SEO and a solid content strategy is crucial for sustainable growth. Quick wins are nice, but long-term success requires building the right foundation.

🎯 E-A-T in Healthcare

For medical/healthcare websites, demonstrating expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness isn't optional—it's essential. Every piece of content must reflect these qualities.

🛡️ Proactive Security

In competitive industries, security threats are inevitable. Having proactive monitoring and rapid response protocols can mean the difference between a minor incident and a catastrophic ranking loss.

The Journey: From Crisis to Breakthrough

The real story behind the numbers - a 5-month rollercoaster of challenges, setbacks, and ultimate success.

⚫ The Darkest Day

It was February. I woke up to a nightmare - my inbox flooded with alerts from Search Console and Google Analytics. The website had been severely hacked. All keyword rankings dropped to zero. Traffic suddenly showed thousands of strange, unknown pages.

Sitting in front of my screen, all my previous SEO knowledge felt meaningless. The client was calling. My team was looking at me, waiting for solutions. The pressure was indescribable. "This is it," I thought. "We're done."

🚨 The First Weeks - "Firefighting" Phase

First, I had to deal with extremely powerful malware - the website was being redirected to Japanese phone sales pages. 30,000 spam links were being fired continuously. Even worse, all official pages lost their index, leaving only hacked pages in Google's collection. The original sitemap disappeared, replaced by a fake sitemap full of garbage links.

The IT team didn't grasp the issue, so they were slow to respond. Every second that passed was another second of reputation damage. I was forced to teach myself, clean every line of code myself, rebuild from scratch. Sleepless nights weren't just from worry - they were from sitting and "dissecting" every file to find deeply hidden malware.

😔 The Struggle - Months 4-5

By this time, traffic had started to recover, but we still couldn't reach our goals. The project timeline was almost up, and despite trying every tactic, the signals weren't coming back. I started questioning myself: "Where did I go wrong?"

Night after night, I analyzed everything from the beginning, checking every metric, every change I'd made. At times, I genuinely doubted my methods. My previous experience didn't seem to apply to this case. It was completely different.

Someone once told me: "Step out of your comfort zone, and you'll learn a lot." Back then, I just nodded along, thinking I understood. But the reality was completely different from what I'd imagined.

Before, doing SEO in a team meant having support, backup plans, and shared responsibility. Now I had to take responsibility for everything - from technical to strategy, from client reports to explaining to my boss why rankings weren't improving yet. I had to know everything, or at least appear like I did.

🎉 The Breakthrough - July

One morning I woke up to a notification: "You Reached XXk Traffic in 30 days" from Google. My first thought: "WTF, what's this? Are we hacked again?" Heart pounding, hands shaking, I opened Search Console.

Checked the rankings and... oh my god! The main keyword had reached top 3. I sat there refreshing several times, thinking Google must be glitching. But no, it was real!

Early July, I thought we were stable, but then we dropped again due to hidden spam links still being fired. At that moment, I felt truly desperate. But then, at the end of July, the real breakthrough came...

Then came the domino effect:

  • 🔥 All other target keywords followed, reaching TOP 5-10
  • 📈 Organic traffic surged 300%
  • 💰 Conversion rate improved 40% compared to before

Time to celebrate! 🐓

💡 Invaluable Lessons

📌 Lesson 1: Technical foundation is everything.

Without a solid foundation, everything else is useless.

📌 Lesson 2: In crisis, don't panic.

Stick to basic principles, take it step by step.

📌 Lesson 3: User experience isn't just a slogan.

Google truly favors pages that genuinely solve user problems.

📌 Lesson 4: Patience in SEO isn't optional.

It's mandatory.

🤔 Looking Back

5 months ago, I thought my career would have a huge black mark. Today, this has become my proudest project.

Not because of the results, but because of the entire journey. Because of the sleepless nights, the moments of wanting to give up but gritting my teeth and continuing.

SEO isn't just about rankings. It's about resilience, about never giving up.

The setup

Tin Hoc Ngoi Sao sells PC components, laptops and gaming gear in Ho Chi Minh City, online and through its own showrooms, on Haravan. The engagement was two months, which rules out most of what SEO normally does. Anything with a payback longer than the contract was not worth starting, so the whole plan was built around what could rank inside weeks.

What was achieved

  • Grew traffic from 1,700 to 11,000 daily visits in two months
  • A +550% lift across the engagement, which is the whole two months and not a monthly rate
  • Full website audit and technical fixes
  • UX work that improved engagement
  • Content deployed against quick-win queries

Why it worked in the time available

  • Technical fixes first, because they pay back fastest
  • Deliberately low-competition keywords rather than head terms
  • Content matched to component and spec queries that already had demand
  • Local SEO for the Vietnam market
  • Internal linking structured to push new pages into the index quickly

A note on the numbers

The site was already doing about 1,700 visits a day when I picked it up, so this was not a launch from nothing. +550% over two months on a base that small is a different achievement from the same percentage on an established site, and the number is the whole engagement rather than a monthly rate. Both of those are worth saying before someone asks.

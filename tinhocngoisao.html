The setup

Tinhocngoisao is a computer training centre in Vietnam. The engagement was two months, which rules out most of what SEO normally does. Anything with a payback longer than the contract was not worth starting, so the whole plan was built around what could rank inside weeks.

What was achieved

  • Grew traffic from 0 to 11K daily visits in two months
  • Sustained a 550% monthly growth rate
  • Full website audit and technical fixes
  • UX work that improved engagement
  • Content deployed against quick-win queries

Why it worked in the time available

  • Technical fixes first, because they pay back fastest
  • Deliberately low-competition keywords rather than head terms
  • Content matched to computer training queries with existing demand
  • Local SEO for the Vietnam market
  • Internal linking structured to push new pages into the index quickly

Technical work

Audit

Full technical audit identifying the critical issues first.

Indexation

Fixed crawlability and indexation problems.

Structure

Reorganized site structure for navigation and crawl.

Speed and mobile

Improved page speed and mobile experience.

Schema

Course schema markup implemented.

A note on the numbers

This was a two month engagement on a small site, so the percentages are large because the starting base was zero. 550% monthly growth from a standing start is a different achievement from the same figure on an established site, and it is worth saying so.
